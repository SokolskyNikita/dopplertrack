# Related projects

Open-source projects that attack the same problem, what they report and what [PLAN.md](../../PLAN.md) takes from them. Reviewed 2026-11-03 from a proposal by Sol; every claim below was checked against the file at the pinned commit.

**Read their numbers as hypotheses, not findings.** Nearly every repository here was created between 2026-11-02 and 2026-11-04, after colsto's video; none is peer reviewed, every figure is self-reported on the project's own development data, and the three in the first table developed on the same Livermore recordings (Pathak 2026). Only two of the rest report a distance on a real recording, both on colsto's Oyster Bay clip against his stated answer with hand-set windows, and none has a blind real result. So a result that holds in one of them is a reason to run the experiment here, never a reason to skip it. Anything we adopt is recorded in [docs/experiments/](../experiments/README.md) with our own measurement.

Licences decide what can be borrowed. MIT allows code with attribution. A repository with no licence file, and DOPPLERGEIST under PolyForm Noncommercial, gives ideas only, reimplemented here. GPL tools (readsb, planefence, noisecapt) are run as tools; none of their code enters this repo.

## What we take, and where it lands

| Idea | What they observed | Our adaptation | Where in PLAN.md | Literature | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Whitening biases frequency | dopplerprint: a 60 s low-quantile floor partly subtracts a slow ridge that sits in one bin for long, 0.4–0.9 bin off, which its fit turned into +2 to +40 % in distance and up to 4 s in CPA time. It now finds tracks on the whitened image and re-measures each frame on the original complex STFT. A 45 s median floor removed the flat asymptotes of slow passes outright | Our `local_whiten` subtracts each bin's median over a window of about two minutes, so a tone that sits in one bin for more than half the window is removed, asymptotes included. Whitening floor and window join Phase 2's model-error perturbations, which the brightness fit can run today, and item 3 finds tracks whitened but measures them on the original STFT | Phase 2, items 2 and 3 | Quinn 1995 (amplitude weights assume unbiased frequency estimates); Kay I eq. 3.41 | Untested here. Not shown to be the cause of the event-2 bias |
| A tracker that stops and restarts | dopplerprint: a global Viterbi path was forced across the whole recording and chained consecutive aircraft. Its tracker is local (Smith–Waterman): the accumulated score is clamped at zero, so a path starts and ends where the tone does, and paths are cut at a weak run over 3 s, a rise of over 4 bins in 3 s, or a hop steeper than the ridge's own slope. echolocate's second-order dynamic programme rewards continuity and penalises slope changes | Two lines in `tracks.ridge()`: clamp at zero and subtract a per-frame bias. That is the stopping rule 6.3 says Iatsenko lacks. It needs an unclipped, zero-mean image, which today's clipped `local_whiten` output isn't. The segment cuts are untested heuristics and get their own tests | Phase 2, item 3 | Iatsenko 2016 §6 names the missing termination rule | Untested here |
| Scores in units of wrong guesses | unheard: almost every try in its coarse (tone, clock) sweep is a wrong guess, so their median and MAD are the recording's own noise for that aircraft, and the score is the best try in those units. Noise alone then peaks at 3–4, because the best of thousands of guesses wins. echolocate: a stationary-tone null was too easy for smooth clutter to beat, so it compares against a fitted linear chirp on the same support | `search` already sweeps ±8 s and ±5 % f0 for every candidate, so the median and spread of those wrong guesses come for free, and a robust z-score against them puts every pair's score on one scale (S2). The chirp alternative is one input to `detect`'s threshold curve, not a gate: short genuine passes look linear | Phase 3 step B; Phase 2 item 6 | Kay II: a maximum over many cells is not a calibrated statistic; confidence is computed after selection | Untested here |
| Remove the source aircraft, keep the traffic | unheard: with the audible aircraft removed from the flight data, another aircraft can fit it. Its "strong" threshold sits 10 % above the worst score with nothing to find, set on exactly those runs | A negative control per confirmed flyover: the right day and box with that flight removed and the surrounding traffic kept. Expected: abstain, or locate from the remaining flyovers; a confident answer from a substituted flight fails. Masking the flight's column in the CPA table avoids a rebuild per variant | 5.1 negatives; Phase 3's failure cases | L2 and the ADS-B-coverage risk in section 7 | Not yet run |
| The Livermore corpus | dopplerprint and unheard tuned on it; echolocate used five clips as a frozen test. dopplerprint reports that a straight-pass fit to the noise-free ADS-B curve misses slant range there by a median 14 %, and by 40–90 % on some passes: model error, not estimator error | On disk since 2026-11-03 (`raw/audio/livermore/`, see the data root's `MANIFEST.md`). Phase 2's main real data for fits and tracks: 40 passes with trajectories and published frequency tracks, in constant and varying speed and height. One group, development or calibration only, never evaluation: one site, one recorder, one month, single passes, and three projects have already tuned on it | 5.6; Phase 2 item 2; decision 2 | Ferguson 2016 §6 on manoeuvring aircraft | Provenance audit pending: the microphone position is not published, both projects reconstructed it from the published offsets, and the WAV start times need checking against the CSV's UTC stamps |
| Altitude provenance | echolocate keeps only geometric heights and refuses to treat pressure altitude as a height; dopplerprint records an altitude source per point (geometric, barometric with the aircraft's own offset, barometric with neighbours' offset, barometric only) | Our loader takes geometric altitude when present and otherwise barometric, without recording which, and ignores the readsb flags for stale points and for a geometric value in slot 3. Geometric ADS-B altitude is WGS-84 ellipsoidal, the Terrarium tiles are orthometric, pressure altitude is neither. A Phase 0 fix keeps the source and honours the flags; the sensitivity test is Phase 2's altitude perturbation | D4; Phase 0; Phase 2 item 2 | None among the papers. The references are readsb's `README-json.md` and the adsb.lol historical-data docs | Fix not yet made |

Two findings of theirs corroborate ours and need no action:
- echolocate found that the steepest received drop happens at the emission-clock CPA, not CPA plus travel time, and that its old proposal was about 18 s off. That matches [timing-conventions](../experiments/2026-11-03-timing-conventions.md) and section 6.1.
- dopplerprint refines each frame's frequency with Candan's three-sample estimator on the Hann DTFT, iterated three times. It is a candidate for Phase 2 item 3's sub-bin estimate alongside quadratic interpolation and Quinn's estimators.

## What we don't take

- dopplerprint's whole pipeline as a dependency. It would replace the parts Phase 2 is meant to measure, and its automatic detection is unbenchmarked even by its author.
- Their accuracy figures as evidence for the method. Both Oyster Bay references are approximate public coordinates, the Livermore microphone is reconstructed and the start times were supplied.
- unheard's thresholds. They were fitted on the same evals they are reported on.
